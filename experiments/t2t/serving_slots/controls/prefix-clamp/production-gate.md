# Paid-prefix seat: fixed-budget production screen

Status: protocol, not results. Freeze before reading candidate generation scores. No warm-up at another budget. No changes to the archived `paid-agreement-v1`.

- **Single change.** One exchange per physical block at the first legal full-width ordinary sampling step: replace its rightmost eligible warm unresolved MASK with its nearest omitted, paid generated prefix row. The prefix row does not gain editing rights that it did not have before. The stock sampler decides only among its eligible actually projected rows. A later promotion, write or EOS never erases a physical charge.
- **Matched screen.** LLaDA 2.1 Q16 and DiffusionGemma Q64; indices `7,13,25,33,55,71`, seed and scorer unchanged. Same card within model. Stock and no-op must agree on full IDs, state transitions, F and Q. At first fork retain all debt, the leftmost unresolved position if selected, the selected right edge, physical width, legal KV and the existing sampler. The swapped-out row must have a resident KV and the new row must actually be projected. No replacement tasks. Identical row sets count as unexposed, not a successful intervention.
- **Screen decision.** Physical failure invalidates that pair and blocks a quality claim. No exposure means no effect for this registration. With valid exposed pairs, prioritize full-90 confirmation when six-case correctness and macro TPF do not decline, with an improvement in at least one of them and no increase in both total F and total Q. This is a resource gate, not an inference of superiority. Otherwise keep the result as a trade-off, not a default.
- **Confirmation.** If qualified, freeze exact runner, scorer, seed and selected policy before the 90. All 90 must complete evaluation under the same failure accounting; never drop failed or long replies. Retain which cases were in the screen. Report original30/new60, outside-screen84, per-dataset and paired gains/losses. TPF is macro; TE is pooled. Record delivered N, F (generation encoder included), projected decoder Q, and compare to same-device paid IBS. Add same-offer Optimus and genuinely matched Flow points separately (nominal offers need observed widths); do not describe historical references as paired single-factor controls.
- **Default decision.** A fixed-budget point can be recommended for joint benefit only if both primary metrics meet the comparator, including the comparison to a strong same-offer baseline, and the physical budget is respected. A net gain due solely to lengthening failed answers is not a computational saving. Publish uncertainty and trade-offs even when one extra correct answer changes the ranking. Do not promote to untested budgets or fit a mixed-budget path from these screens.

The hard-budget exchange also has an opportunity cost: one fewer MASK is sampled on that step. It is distinct from the scratch K/V counterfactual and from filling previously unused capacity.
