# Frozen rerank: recent visible token changes

The top-eight diagnostic motivates a second choice *within* the existing outside shortlist, not spending eight Qs. Keep the trained Q8 gate, victim, top-eight shortlist, and physical one-seat policy fixed. This is an exploratory follow-up on the same frozen30, not fresh independent validation.

The single new signal uses the **previous completed transition**. For the candidate i, take changed neighbours j at distances 1–8 in this block. Average the projected input vector change `U(after_j) - U(before_j)` with weights `1 / abs(j-i)`, normalizing by the total weight; use zero when no such neighbour exists. MASK is a valid input-vector ID. The two features are the norm of this mean and its dot product with `U(current_i)`. All vectors use the same cached 16-dimensional input-embedding projection. No logits, current-step projections or KV drift, future token, or reference answer is a predictor feature. Log odds from the frozen old ranker are the **fixed offset**; estimate only two slopes on the original disjoint calibration24, L2=2, no intercept. Training uses physical outside top-eight candidates on gate-open states. No change to the gate or alternative radii, dimensions, thresholds, features or training penalties.

The primary endpoint is strict old-edit hits among the one selected row, separated by MASK vs ordinary old victim. Advance to a sparse rollout only with at least 19 MASK-victim hits (frozen baseline 16), and at least 8 old-victim hits (baseline 8), with identical gate openings, seats and victim admissions. A native-replay pass would justify a rollout, not a claim about improved quality or TPF.
